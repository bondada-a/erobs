Hardware and perception

EROBS plans with a model of the arm, attached tool, and surrounding cell. Drivers execute those plans; camera measurements provide targets for vision-guided tasks.

The CMS configuration describes a UR5e with a wrist-mounted Zivid camera. It includes models and settings for Hand-E, ePick, 2FG7, the pipettor, and the no-tool state (none). LIX has separate model and planning packages, but its deployment configuration is incomplete; see beamline adaptation.
