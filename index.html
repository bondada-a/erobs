EROBS documentation

ROS 2 Jazzy · beamline robotics

EROBS (Extensible Robotic Beamline Scientist) is a ROS 2 application for robotic sample handling. It executes JSON task scripts that coordinate a UR arm, interchangeable tools, and 3D vision.
